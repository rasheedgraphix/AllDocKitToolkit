100% Privacy Focused

Privacy Policy for AllDocKit

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Developer & Publisher: Rasheed Graphix (Hafiz Nouman Rasheed)

1. 100% Offline Local Processing

AllDocKit is built with an offline-first privacy architecture. All file conversions, PDF manipulations (merging, splitting, compression, page reordering), image processing, and text recognition occur strictly inside your device's memory. Your documents, photos, and files are NEVER uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external server.

2. User Authentication & Account Data

When you choose to create an account or sign in with Google or Email:

  • We securely store your email address and basic profile name through Google Firebase Authentication solely for the purpose of synchronizing your subscription status, trial period, and purchased Pro license keys across your devices.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal contact information with any third party.

3. Offline Guest Usage

AllDocKit can be used completely offline as a guest without creating an account or providing any personal information.

4. Payments & Licensing

Payment verification information provided for manual subscriptions (such as transaction IDs) is utilized exclusively to validate and issue legitimate software license keys.
